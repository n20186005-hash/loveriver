## Typical events

Love River and Love River Bay host seasonal light displays, Dragon Boat Festival races, waterfront performances, markets and concerts at Kaohsiung Music Center. Major events can change traffic, walking access and boat operations. A regular sightseeing route may not work unchanged on event days.

## Three sources to check

1. The [Kaohsiung tourism events calendar](https://khh.travel/en/event/calendar/).
2. Kaohsiung Music Center's current program and venue notices.
3. Kaohsiung MRT, light rail and city traffic-control notices.

For boat trips, also check the operator's pier and service updates, including cancellations.

## Planning around crowds

Arrive early, reduce transfers between districts and schedule dinner before peak times. After a concert, light-rail platforms can be busy. You may wait before leaving or walk to another station using well-lit public routes, but do not take isolated waterfront shortcuts.

## Dragon boat and water-based events

Race courses and temporary closures can affect boats, riverbanks and paths beneath bridges. Spectate only in designated public areas. Never assume this year's access arrangements match photographs from an earlier event.

## Is Love River worthwhile without an event?

Absolutely. Its bridges, riverside walks, boat trips and harbor views remain the main attractions. Quieter evenings often make walking and photography more enjoyable.

## Final check

Always consult the latest organizer, transport-operator and waterfront notices on the day you travel.
