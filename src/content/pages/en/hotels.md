## Cianjin: closest to the classic river walk

Ideal for first-time visitors who want to return to their accommodation on foot after seeing the lights. Do not rely only on an accommodation listing's “Love River” tag: inspect its actual entrance and walking route on a map.

## Yancheng: Pier-2, snacks and old-town streets

Convenient for combining Pier-2, Dagang Bridge and Love River Bay. Accommodation varies considerably by street, as do evening ambience and actual walking distance to the waterfront.

## Love River Bay: best for performances

A good base for concerts and large events around Kaohsiung Music Center. Check the route between your venue exit and hotel entrance; crowds, transport demand and prices can change on event nights.

## Kaohsiung Main Station: prioritize transfers

For one-night stays with onward train travel, staying near the main station can be more practical than chasing a river view. Take the MRT to the waterfront instead of pulling luggage along it.

## Zuoying: early high-speed rail departures

Consider Zuoying if you have an early HSR train the next morning. Leave sufficient time after your Love River evening for transfers; avoid relying exclusively on the last service.

## Booking checklist

Verify the nearest station, lifts, baggage storage, cancellation terms and real entrance. Live rates and availability are determined by the accommodation provider or booking platform at checkout.
