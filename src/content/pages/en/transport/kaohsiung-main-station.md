## Choose your destination before an exit

Kaohsiung Main Station is a transport interchange, not a riverside entrance. Search your exact final landmark in your map app. Travellers carrying luggage may prefer to store bags before walking by the river.

## Three different destinations

- **Love Boat and classic river:** MRT **O4 Qianjin** after changing lines at Formosa Boulevard.
- **Love River Bay and Music Center:** light-rail stops around **C10 Glory Pier** and **C11 Love Pier**.
- **Heart of Love River:** light-rail **C24**, farther upstream.

## Travel sequence

Follow station signs to the Red Line, plan your MRT or light-rail interchange based on the destination, then use marked pedestrian crossings from the final stop. Verify current routes, service changes and final departures using the transit operator.

## Before departing

Check whether special events affect the light rail, whether your chosen attraction is open, and how you will return. For an easy first visit, [the downtown waterfront guide](/en/areas/downtown-love-river/) can help plan the walking portion.

## Extend your visit

Allow time for the Museum of History, Yancheng food streets or Love River Bay if your schedule permits. Avoid planning a long upstream-to-harbour walk without checking distances.
