## Leaving the airport

Kaohsiung International Airport connects directly to the MRT **Red Line**. For classic downtown Love River, ride to **Formosa Boulevard**, change to the **Orange Line** and get off at **O4 Qianjin**. For the harbour bay or upstream Heart of Love River, plan a light-rail connection according to your final landmark.

## Travelling with luggage

If your hotel is near a station, leave heavy bags there before exploring. Riverside steps, bridges and shared walking paths are not ideal for rolling large suitcases for long distances. On a late arrival, a taxi to a specific attraction can be simpler; fares depend on the actual journey and meter.

## Love River is not a single stop

For a sightseeing cruise, choose **Love Boat Ambassador Pier**; for Kaohsiung Music Center, choose Love River Bay around **C10–C11**; and for the upstream twin lakes, choose **C24 Heart of Love River**. Each destination has a different optimal approach.

## Short arrival-day plan

If you land in the afternoon, check in first and keep one manageable waterfront outing for the evening. A downtown walk with an optional boat ride is often easier than trying to visit all three districts. Check [public transport instructions](/en/transport/) for your final transfer.
