## Which station should you use?

| Stop | Main destination | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **C10 Glory Pier** | Open harbour views, Music Center side | Sunset and night photography |
| **C11 Love Pier** | Love Pier and waterfront paths | First walk around the bay |
| **C12 Dayi Pier-2** | Pier-2 warehouses and Dagang Bridge | Culture and harbour scenery |
| **C24 Heart of Love River** | Upstream twin-lake landscape | Quiet walking and water views |

## A one-way walk saves energy

Start at C10 and finish around C12, or walk in reverse. You can pass Glory Pier, Kaohsiung Music Center, Love Pier and the Pier-2 area without returning to your starting stop. Use the light rail again at the end.

## How this relates to the downtown Love Boat

The classic Ambassador Pier cruise area lies on the downtown river and is often better reached via **MRT O4 Qianjin**. Do not assume that **C11 Love Pier** is the Love Boat boarding point simply because the names sound similar.

## Service times and last trains

Timetables, headways and disruption notices can change. Consult the [official Kaohsiung Light Rail site](https://www.krtc.com.tw/KLRT/) for the actual service and last train before you leave, especially after a concert.
