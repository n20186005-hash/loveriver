## First step after the high-speed train

Follow signs to **MRT Red Line R16 Zuoying**. Do not confuse the HSR, TRA Xinzuoying and MRT entrances when looking for a riverside destination. If you only have half a day in Kaohsiung, select one main river section rather than racing through all three.

## Love Boat and the classic downtown banks

Ride the Red Line to **Formosa Boulevard**, transfer to the Orange Line and leave at **O4 Qianjin**. Walk towards Ambassador Pier for the classic Love Boat area and bridge views. The [Love Boat guide](/en/boats/love-boat/) explains how to allow time for a cruise.

## Love River Bay, Music Center and Pier-2

For modern harbour scenery, plan a transfer towards light-rail **C10 Glory Pier**, **C11 Love Pier** or **C12 Dayi Pier-2**, depending on your chosen walking direction. Check the current transit network for the most convenient connection.

## Heart of Love River

For the upstream lakes, use light-rail **C24 Heart of Love River**. It is a separate destination and is not a short continuation of the downtown river walk.

## Protect your HSR return journey

Leave a generous buffer for light-rail frequency, MRT transfers and walking back to the platform. Cruise queues and special events can delay your plans. Check current service notices and avoid scheduling the last possible connection after an evening outing.
