## Choose a destination, not just “Love River parking”

Heart of Love River, the downtown cruise area and Love River Bay are too far apart to treat as one parking zone. First decide where your visit will end, then compare legal public or commercial car parks around that specific landmark.

| Main activity | Landmark for a parking search | After parking |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Love Boat and classic night view | Love Boat Ambassador Pier | Walk Hedong or Hexi Road |
| Music Center and Love Pier | Kaohsiung Music Center | Explore the harbour waterfront |
| Pier-2 and Dagang Bridge | Dayi Pier-2 warehouses | Walk rather than drive between nearby stops |
| Heart of Love River | Heart of Love River | Walk around the lakes |

## Weekends and special events

Parking demand and road controls can change around concerts and festivals. Allow extra time, check temporary signage and consider MRT or light rail if a major event is scheduled. Avoid stopping in restricted loading zones or blocking residents' access.

## Rates and opening hours

Fees, height limits, payment methods and opening times vary. Check the exact parking facility's current details before driving in. Do not rely on a general guide for real-time availability; keep an alternative facility in mind if your first choice is full.
