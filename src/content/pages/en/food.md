## Why choose an area before a restaurant?

Opening days, lines and business conditions change rapidly. Picking a neighborhood along your walking route, then checking currently operating restaurants, is usually more practical than following an old list of dozens of names.

## Yancheng: snacks and historic streets

Pair lunch here with Pier-2 or a half-day Love River trip. Eat before visiting galleries in the afternoon. Some traditional businesses close earlier in the evening or have irregular days off, so check the same-day schedule.

## Cianjin: easiest before or after a boat trip

For the Ambassador Hotel boarding area and classic downtown riverside, eating in Cianjin minimizes cross-town travel. Dinner after a cruise is convenient, but allow waiting time on weekends and holidays.

## Love River Bay: plan around events

Concerts and festivals can make nearby restaurants very busy. Work out your entry and exit stations first, then choose a meal near the final transit stop rather than traveling out of your way for a viral recommendation.

## Four on-the-ground checks

1. Confirm the business is actually open today.
2. Compare the walking detour with the sunset time you would lose.
3. Read the restaurant's own recent announcements.
4. Never schedule uncertain waiting time immediately before a boat or concert.

## Pair meals with your itinerary

Skip a sit-down meal on a two-hour walk. For half a day, eat in Yancheng before the waterfront. On a full-day trip, have lunch in Yancheng and dinner in Cianjin or near your final station.
