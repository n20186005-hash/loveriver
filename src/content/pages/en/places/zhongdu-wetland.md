## Background and management

Zhongdu Wetlands Park is part of Love River's ecological corridor, where habitat-oriented landscaping supports environmental education and city recreation. Kaohsiung Tourism notes that the park received a global construction excellence award in **2012**. Municipal agencies oversee public-park upkeep; follow their current notices for temporary repairs, guided activities and restricted facilities.

## Landscapes and habitat design

Unlike the busy harbor, Zhongdu's appeal lies in timber structures, walkways, bridges, platforms, layers of vegetation and waterbird habitat. Wooden interpretive facilities and pavilions respond to the natural setting. Paths create different vantage points across water and mangrove areas without requiring visitors to enter sensitive habitat.

## Suggested visit route

1. Navigate to **Zhongdu Wetlands Park** by its full name and identify a currently open entrance.
2. Read the interpretive displays to understand habitat restoration and local rules.
3. Follow boardwalks or bridges to observe water, plants and birds without leaving designated paths.
4. Stop at a safe viewpoint for reflections, timber architecture and distant city scenery.
5. Return along an open route; use separate transport if continuing to Heart of Love River.

## Transport and parking

This park is not next to the downtown Love Boat pier and is not the same as **C24 Heart of Love River**. Check current city-bus connections and the final walking route to the entrance, or find legal parking nearby. Do not navigate merely to a generic “Love River” location. Consult the [Kaohsiung transport overview](/en/transport/).

## Photography, families and wildlife etiquette

Wooden bridges frame water and habitat layers; the interpretive buildings offer interesting timber and green-space details. Morning angled light brings out vegetation and reflections. Binoculars are better than approaching birds. Do not feed or capture animals, disturb plants or leave trails. Bring nonslip shoes and insect protection, and avoid the water during thunderstorms.
