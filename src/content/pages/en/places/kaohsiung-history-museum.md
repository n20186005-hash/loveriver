## From city hall to history museum

The building once housed Kaohsiung's city government and became a museum after the administration moved in **1992**. Its civic history also intersects with local memories of the February 28 Incident. The building was designated a municipal historic monument in **2004**. Its reuse makes the architecture an exhibit in its own right.

## Architecture and observation points

Across the street, view the **pale-green facade**, elevated central tower and symmetrical wings as one composition. Then look more closely at windows, rooflines, traditional tiled elements and the way the roof forms meet the public-building proportions. Photograph from the sidewalk, never from the roadway.

## Management, hours and admission

The museum manages collections, research, exhibitions and educational programs. Kaohsiung Tourism lists its address as **No. 272 Zhongzheng 4th Road**, general visiting hours **Tuesday–Sunday 09:00–17:00**, Monday closure and usually free general admission. Special exhibitions, holiday exceptions and events should be checked with the museum.

## Half-day walking route

1. Leave **O2 Yanchengpu** and walk through the neighborhood to the museum.
2. Observe the symmetrical front and central tower before going inside.
3. After the exhibitions, walk to Love River's western bank and compare the historic building with the modern river corridor.
4. Return to [Yancheng's streets](/en/places/yancheng-old-streets/) for food or coffee; add Pier-2 if time allows.

## Families, photography and rainy days

Ask children why an old city hall might become a museum. Outside, a frontal composition emphasizes symmetry; oblique views reveal roof depth. Exhibition areas set their own photography restrictions. In rain, begin indoors and save the river walk until paths are less slippery.

## Where to stay and what to see nearby

Yanchengpu or other Orange Line locations make it easier to combine the museum, river and Pier-2. [Holy Rosary Cathedral](/en/places/holy-rosary-cathedral/) can add another perspective on local history. See the [accommodation guide](/en/hotels/).
