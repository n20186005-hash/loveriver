## A simple way to visit

Get off at **C11 Love Pier** and first enjoy the view across the harbor toward Kaohsiung Music Center. Then choose a one-way direction: north toward downtown Love River or west toward Dayi warehouses and Dagang Bridge.

## Best time of day

Late afternoon to evening gives you both a clear view of the harbor layout and reflections after the lights come on. Concerts and other events may bring stages, crowds or temporary restrictions; weekdays are often better for a quiet walk.

## How to get here

Take the light rail to C11. If arriving on foot from downtown, follow Love River south and use signed crossings. Temporary arrangements during events should take priority over an old map or walking recommendation.

## What to do on site

You do not need to spend the whole afternoon at the pier. It works best as a junction between the music-center waterfront, the city riverbanks and Pier-2. Choose one direction and avoid repeatedly walking the same stretch.

## Photography and safety

The harbor, concert architecture and light rail create layered city compositions. Never block station platforms, roads or walking routes, and stay behind waterfront safety barriers.

## Nearby stops

Combine the pier with [Kaohsiung Music Center](/en/places/kaohsiung-music-center/), Glory Pier, Pier-2 and Dagang Bridge. With only an hour, prioritize the music-center exterior; with half a day, continue toward Pier-2.
