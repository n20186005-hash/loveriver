## History and management

Glory Pier stands where Love River meets Kaohsiung Harbor. Kaohsiung Tourism describes the area as the former **Pier No. 13**, which was used to transport military personnel and supplies associated with Kinmen and Matsu. Its conversion into a public waterfront reflects the wider transformation of the port. Harbor operating spaces and events are subject to the appropriate port and municipal management arrangements.

## What to notice

This is less about a single building than the open water, quay lines, skyline and surrounding music-center architecture. Compare vertical city towers with the horizontal harbor. The changing balance between sky and water is especially striking before sunset.

## One-way waterfront route

1. Exit **C10 Glory Pier** and follow signs to the waterside viewing area.
2. Look across the water toward the skyline and harbor activity.
3. Walk toward the distinctive buildings of [Kaohsiung Music Center](/en/places/kaohsiung-music-center/).
4. Continue to Love Pier and optionally toward Pier-2; depart via a different light-rail stop to avoid backtracking.

## Photography and safety

The open pier is suitable for wide harbor views. Along the music-center side, frame buildings with the water at blue hour. Sea breezes can be strong, so secure equipment. Do not cross barriers, enter working berths or approach mooring gear. The harbor is not a swimming area.

## Families, food and accommodation

Use the pier as a short scenic stop before indoor activities and food near the music center or Pier-2. Event-goers can consult the [after-concert itinerary](/en/itineraries/after-concert/). For accommodation, prioritize nearby transit connections instead of assuming every harbor-view listing is walkable.
