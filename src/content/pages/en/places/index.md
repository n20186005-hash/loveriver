## The easiest sightseeing order

**Downtown Love River → Love Pier → Kaohsiung Music Center → Dagang Bridge → Pier-2 Art Center**

This waterfront sequence generally moves from northeast to southwest and works well with walking and light rail. Reverse it if you begin in Yancheng, but leave from a different station to avoid backtracking.

## Love Pier

The point where the river opens into the harbor, served by light rail C11. Stop for reflections and continue toward the music center or use it as a link between downtown and Pier-2. [Read the Love Pier guide](/en/places/true-love-pier/).

## Kaohsiung Music Center

The bay's most distinctive architectural landmark. Its outdoor waterfront deserves a visit even without a concert, while indoor access depends on performances and venue notices. [Explore the music center](/en/places/kaohsiung-music-center/).

## Dagang Bridge

A movable pedestrian bridge connecting warehouse districts and creating a striking harbor view. Bridge rotations, maintenance and closures follow port notices. [Plan a bridge visit](/en/places/dagou-bridge/).

## Pier-2 Art Center

Warehouses, exhibitions and public art spread across a larger area than many visitors expect. Choose one or two areas instead of sacrificing sunset time to see every building. [See the Pier-2 guide](/en/places/pier-2-art-center/).

## Around the downtown river

Kaohsiung Museum of History, the waterfront parks and Holy Rosary Cathedral can fit into the same district walk. Indoor venue schedules and religious etiquette are different from the freely accessible riverbanks.

## How much should you include?

With two hours, concentrate on downtown and the bay. With half a day, add Dagang Bridge and Pier-2. A full day leaves time for a museum and Yancheng food. More stops do not necessarily make a better visit; save time for the light near sunset.
