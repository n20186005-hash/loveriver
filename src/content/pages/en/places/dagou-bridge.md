## Why include Dagang Bridge?

It is both a photographic landmark and a useful pedestrian connection across the port area. The bridge links Pier-2's warehouse districts with the Love River Bay itinerary. Seeing it while moving between sights saves time.

## Watching the rotation

The movable bridge accommodates port operations. Rotation demonstrations can be rescheduled or suspended due to maintenance, ship movements, weather or special events. Check current Kaohsiung Port information before making a special trip to see it rotate.

## Best photo opportunities

The white bridge reads clearly against the harbor by day. Near sunset, wider shots capture the water and changing sky; at blue hour, lights add atmosphere. Strong winds are possible on the span, so secure hats and camera equipment.

## Transport and walking route

From **C12 Dayi Pier-2** or **C13 Penglai Pier-2**, follow local signs to Dagang Bridge. Coming from Kaohsiung Music Center, walk along the waterfront toward Pier-2 and cross when open. For half a day, combine one warehouse area, the bridge and [Love River Bay](/en/areas/love-river-bay/).

## Safety and accessibility

Obey closing lines during rotation and vessel movements. Never attempt a last-minute crossing. Avoid standing still in the middle of busy pedestrian lanes, and keep the route clear for wheelchairs and other visitors.
