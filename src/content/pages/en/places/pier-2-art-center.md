## Understanding the scale of Pier-2

Pier-2 is not one indoor museum. Historic warehouse groups now hold galleries, shops and exhibition spaces, with public artworks and walkways between them. First-time visitors who also want to see Love River should avoid scheduling every building.

## Which area to choose?

**Dayi** connects conveniently with Love River Bay and Dagang Bridge. **Penglai** extends toward railway heritage and larger warehouse views. Exhibitions and ticket rules vary, so check [Pier-2's official program](https://pier2.org/) before choosing.

## Half-day route

Start from Yanchengpu MRT, explore the Dayong or Dayi area, cross [Dagang Bridge](/en/places/dagou-bridge/), continue to Love River Bay and depart at C11 Love Pier. On a hot day, schedule indoor galleries first and reach the harbor closer to sunset.

## Visiting with children or a stroller

Outdoor areas offer room to explore, but sun exposure, surfaces and crowd levels vary. Keep the route short and use calmer walkways when a market or event attracts a crowd. Check step-free access for the exhibition venue you intend to enter.

## Photography and etiquette

Many outdoor artworks are photographable, but galleries may limit flash, tripods, filming or commercial use. Obey posted rules, do not climb installations and never stand on railway or light-rail infrastructure for a photograph.

## What next?

If you have energy, walk the harbor after crossing the bridge. Otherwise ride the light rail to C10 Glory Pier or C11 Love Pier and reserve only one sunset stop.
