## Privacy policy

**Last updated: 11 November 2026**

This policy applies to [loveriver.org](https://www.loveriver.org/), an independent guide to Love River, nearby attractions, transport and itineraries in Kaohsiung. It explains what information may be processed when you visit and what choices you have. This is not an official city-government or attraction-operator website.

## Information we may process

### Website delivery and security

Our hosting and network providers may process IP addresses, request times, browser details, connection records and technical errors to deliver pages, protect the site and resolve problems. Their policies and security requirements affect how long these records are kept.

### Optional analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate visits and improve site content. **The analytics script loads only after you expressly accept it.** You can read the guide without consenting. Analytics may process aggregate page activity, device types and traffic sources; Google may also process technical identifiers according to its policies.

### Information you voluntarily send

We do not currently offer member accounts or accept direct travel payments. If you contact us, the contact details in your message are used to answer your enquiry, not automatically added to a marketing list.

## Cookies, local storage and offline use

Your browser stores the `loveriver-analytics-consent` preference so the site remembers whether you accepted or rejected analytics. If you consent, Google Analytics may use analytical cookies or related storage. A service worker may also cache pages to make them available offline and speed up loading. This cache is not an advertising tracker.

You can [change or withdraw consent](/en/cookie-settings/) whenever you wish, or clear cookies, local storage and offline caches in your browser settings.

## External websites and embedded content

The guide links to Google Maps, government tourism pages, transit operators and other sources. Some pages may embed a Google map. Third parties may process connection and device information when you open their sites or load their embeds, under their own privacy policies.

## Purposes and sharing

Technical data is used to serve and secure the website. Analytics data, where consented to, helps us assess content and navigation. We do not operate a business selling personal data. Hosting, analytics and embedding providers may process the data necessary to perform their services.

## Retention, international processing and security

Your consent choice stays in your browser until you change it or delete site data. Hosting and analytics providers determine their technical retention periods under their configurations and policies. Cloud processing may occur outside your country. We take reasonable security precautions, but no internet transmission is completely risk-free.

## Your choices and rights

- [Accept, reject or withdraw optional analytics](/en/cookie-settings/).
- Delete cookies, local site data or website permissions in your browser.
- Send a request about data directly controlled by this website through its technical-support contact. Requests about data held by a third-party provider may also need to be directed to that provider.

## Updates to this policy

If site functions, providers or applicable requirements change, we may revise this policy and update the date shown above. We will try to highlight material changes on the website.
