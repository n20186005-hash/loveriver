## How it differs from downtown Love River

Heart of Love River sits upstream, with two artificial water basins and connecting bridges suggesting a heart shape. It is neither the Ambassador Pier cruise area nor Love River Bay beside the Music Center. Its evening appeal is the quieter glow of the lake edges and reflected lights.

## Walking from C24

Take the light rail to **C24 Heart of Love River** and follow pedestrian signs to the water. Walk the main paths, enjoy the bridges and observe the outline of the basins from different angles. There is no need to rush every side path.

## Night photography precautions

Stay on illuminated public routes and photograph from safe positions behind railings. Paving may be slippery after rain. Never climb barriers for an overhead photograph; lighting can change due to maintenance or events.

## How long to allow

Allow roughly **45–90 minutes**, excluding travel between districts. If you also want a Love Boat ride, plan it as a separate portion of your day.
