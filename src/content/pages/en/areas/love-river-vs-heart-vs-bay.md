## Quick comparison

| Feature | Downtown Love River | Love River Bay | Heart of Love River |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main experience | Cruises, classic night views | Harbour buildings, sunset, events | Twin lakes, ecology, cycling |
| Nearest station | MRT O4 Qianjin / O2 Yanchengpu | Light rail C10–C12 | Light rail C24 |
| Suggested time | 1.5–2.5 hours | 2–3 hours | 45–90 minutes |
| Best for | First-time visitors, families | Photography, architecture, events | Cycling, running, quieter walks |
| Walk between areas? | Walk to the bay | Walk to downtown | Use public transport |

## If you want a Love Boat or gondola

Go to **downtown Love River**. Search the actual Ambassador Pier boarding point, rather than navigating to “Heart of Love River” simply because the name sounds romantic.

## If you want Kaohsiung Music Center

Choose **Love River Bay**. Start from light-rail Glory Pier or Love Pier, walk the harbour edge and extend towards Pier-2 and Dagang Bridge.

## If you want to cycle or run

Choose **Heart of Love River** upstream. Its twin lakes and connecting paths suit slower recreation, not the traditional tourist-boat experience.

## Practical first-visit itineraries

- **Two hours:** Downtown Love River → Love River Bay.
- **Half a day:** Yancheng or the History Museum → downtown boat trip → bay sunset.
- **Full day:** Pier-2 and Dagang Bridge → Love River Bay → downtown after dark. Add the upstream Heart on another day.

## Common location mistake

Visitors sometimes arrive at Love Pier expecting the Ambassador Pier Love Boat, or travel to Heart of Love River expecting the classic night cruise. **Choose the experience first and the station second**: similar names do not mean adjacent places.
