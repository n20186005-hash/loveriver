## Three classic compositions

| Subject | Suggested area | Composition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boats and bridges | Downtown Love River | Let the bridge frame the water channel |
| Harbour and contemporary buildings | Love River Bay | Leave open water around the Music Center's lines |
| Water and bridges | Heart of Love River | Use a wider view to show the shape of the basins |

## Daylight, sunset or night?

Daylight is best for architectural detail and colour. Around sunset, watch the changing warmth of the water. At night, seek reflections from illuminated buildings and bridges. Rain may produce unusual reflections on streets and water, but avoid exposed waterfronts during thunderstorms.

## Shooting with a phone

Clean the lens and keep the horizon level. Start at the normal focal length before using ultra-wide. A long night-mode exposure can blur moving boats; embrace some motion rather than expecting every part of the scene to be sharp.

## Photography etiquette

Bridge decks and shared paths must remain passable for wheelchairs, bicycles and pedestrians. Do not place tripods in others' way or enter restricted harbour facilities. For a longer outing, see the [Love River photography itinerary](/en/itineraries/photography/).
