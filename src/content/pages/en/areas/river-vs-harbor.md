## Understand the geography first

Love River runs through Kaohsiung and its character changes along the way. The downtown stretch is associated with tourist boats and illuminated bridges. Love River Bay marks the open water by the river mouth and Kaohsiung Harbour. Farther upstream, Heart of Love River is a separate heart-shaped water landscape.

## Three-area comparison

| Area | Most suitable for | Transport |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Downtown Love River | Night cruises and bridge views | MRT O4 Qianjin |
| Love River Bay | Harbour, Music Center, Pier-2 | Light rail C10–C12 |
| Heart of Love River | Lakes, greenery, relaxed walks | Light rail C24 |

## Which should you choose first?

For an hour after dark and a classic Love River atmosphere, walk downtown. If you are already at Pier-2 during the day, continue to the bay. For a quiet walk with shaped lakes and bridges, visit Heart of Love River separately. These places are not within one short walking distance simply because their names resemble one another.
