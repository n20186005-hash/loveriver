## Spring: take a relaxed walk

Combine the downtown river and Yancheng streets in a half-day, adding indoor exhibitions or a meal along the way. Check rain and UV forecasts instead of assuming a season guarantees fine weather.

## Summer: avoid the midday sun

Kaohsiung can be very hot in summer. Focus walking on the morning and evening, carry drinking water, sun protection and insect repellent. Sudden afternoon rain may make bridge surfaces and waterside steps slippery.

## Autumn: sunset into the evening

Love River Bay is a good location to watch the harbour and Music Center change colour as night approaches. Actual sunset time and direction vary daily, so check the forecast rather than following a fixed clock time.

## Winter: prepare for waterfront wind

Even on pleasant days, evenings near open water may feel windy. Bring a light layer for a boat ride or extended night photography. Look out for event-related transport changes and consult the [best visiting time guide](/en/best-time-to-visit/).
