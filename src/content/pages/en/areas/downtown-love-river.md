## Why start here on your first visit?

The scale of the river, the close bridges and the illuminated banks make this the classic Love River scene. Ambassador Pier is a principal boarding area for sightseeing trips, including the Love Boat and gondola experiences. You can enjoy the atmosphere without having to explore the entire length of the river first.

The Love Boat emphasises sightseeing commentary, while the gondola is a more intimate experience. Compare them in the [English boat guide](/en/boats/cruise-comparison/) before choosing a departure.

## How to get there

Walk from MRT **O4 Qianjin** or **O2 Yanchengpu**. If you plan to cruise, navigate directly to the Love Boat's Ambassador Pier boarding area or the specific ticket counter. Searching for only “Love River” may send you to an entirely different stretch.

## Walking the waterfront

Check boarding times near Ambassador Pier before setting off. Explore Hedong Road while you wait, then continue towards Wufu Road and Love River Bay after your cruise. This keeps the route moving rather than retracing every step and lets you see the banks change from daylight to night.

Both sides offer different compositions: one may frame the opposite skyline, while the other combines paths, trees and water. Use marked crossings and traffic signals when changing banks.

## Best time to visit

Arrive before sunset. Daylight helps with orientation, blue hour outlines bridges and buildings, and evening light creates river reflections. Weekends sometimes bring markets or performances, but also bigger crowds and longer boarding waits.

## What to see nearby

Northward, consider the Kaohsiung Museum of History and the waterfront parks. Southward, continue towards Holy Rosary Cathedral, Love Pier and the Kaohsiung Music Center. For a two-hour visit, heading south into Love River Bay is a practical choice.

## Visitor notes

This is a public waterfront, not a fenced attraction. Keep children close to the water and leave space for other users while photographing. Boat services may change due to weather or waterway events, so check the operator on the day.
