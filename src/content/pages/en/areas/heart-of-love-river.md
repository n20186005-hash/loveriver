## What is Heart of Love River?

Two artificial lakes, connected by bridges, create the landscape's heart-shaped impression. Shallower waters contribute ecological functions, while surrounding paths connect to recreational cycling routes. Compared with the busy downtown cruise stretch, this area feels more local and relaxed.

## Easiest way to arrive

Ride the light rail to **C24 Heart of Love River**. Check pedestrian exits and crossings before heading towards the bank of your choice. Do not try to walk here directly from the classic downtown cruise area; use public transport and allow transfer time.

## Suggested visit lengths

- **45 minutes:** Follow the main water edge and bridges, photograph the evening view and return.
- **90 minutes:** Walk around both lakes at a slower pace and watch the water and wildlife.
- **Half a day:** Include the attraction within a longer riverside bicycle route.

An arrival in daylight lets you understand the two basins before their lighting comes on.

## Best time and atmosphere

Early mornings and evenings are comfortable for walking, running and cycling. At night, bridges and reflections create the strongest mood. Summer midday shade varies along the paths; avoid prolonged heat exposure.

## Heart of Love River or Love River Bay?

Choose Love River Bay for contemporary harbour architecture, sunset and events. Choose Heart of Love River for gentler exercise, a quieter waterside and fewer tourist crowds. Both can be attractive at night, but they are different locations and experiences.

## Safety notes

Although the public waterfront is generally accessible, lighting and crowds vary by segment. Stay on main paths when alone at night, slow down when cycling near pedestrians and watch for slippery surfaces or temporary closures after heavy rain.
