## Downtown: best for a first visit

Ambassador Pier, Love Boat, gondola trips and classic illuminated bridges cluster around this section. Reach it from MRT **O4 Qianjin** or **O2 Yanchengpu**. Allow **1.5–2.5 hours**.

## Love River Bay: architecture and sunset

Love Pier, Glory Pier and Kaohsiung Music Center surround a wider harbour view. Walk one-way between light-rail stops **C10–C12**, then continue to Dagang Bridge and Pier-2 if time permits.

## Heart of Love River: ecology and cycling

Farther north and upstream, the twin-lake setting begins near light-rail **C24 Heart of Love River**. Set aside **45–90 minutes** for a walk or a separate half-day cycling trip.

## Can you combine the areas?

Downtown and the bay can be linked on foot. Heart of Love River is better reached by light rail and planned separately. Do not try to cover all three during a two-hour visit.
