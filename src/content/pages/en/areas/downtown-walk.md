## Reach the river from Qianjin Station

Orange Line station **O4 Qianjin** was formerly called City Council. On leaving the station, set “Love Boat Ambassador Pier” as a precise waterside destination. Once there, decide whether to walk upstream or downstream; bridges connect the banks, so you need not cross the road repeatedly for each view.

## Hedong or Hexi Road?

The two banks have different perspectives. Look across to the buildings and lights from one side, or frame the bridges and boat traffic from the other. Observe the sun and pedestrian traffic before choosing. For strollers and mobility aids, use level pavements instead of waterside steps.

## What to notice along the way

Look for how bridges, river planting and the water channel shape the cityscape. In daylight, focus on water and greenery; after dark, watch boat lights reflected beneath the bridges. Those cruising should first check [Love Boat fares](/en/boats/tickets/). Walking the public path requires no admission ticket.

## Continue to Yancheng

The western bank links naturally with the [Kaohsiung Museum of History](/en/places/kaohsiung-history-museum/) and the streets of Yancheng. On hot days, combine an indoor museum stop with an evening waterside stroll. Pedestrians and cyclists may share sections of the route.
