## How the three night scenes differ

| Area | What you will see | Transport starting point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Downtown river | Illuminated bridges, boats, reflections | MRT O4 Qianjin |
| Love River Bay | Wide water and Music Center architecture | LRT C10–C12 |
| Heart of Love River | Twin-lake shapes, bridges and lakeside lights | LRT C24 |

## Best option for a first visit

With just an hour, do not chase all three places. Walk from Qianjin to the downtown waterfront, photograph a bridge and the passing boats, and return comfortably. With half a day, consider a one-way walk from **C10 to C12** around Love River Bay and extend to [Dagang Bridge](/en/places/dagou-bridge/) and Pier-2.

## Night photography tips

Choose water that reflects a visible source of light, and include a bridge or railing to establish a sense of place. Secure phones and camera straps in the wind. Do not place a tripod where it blocks a bridge or walkway.

## Staying safe after dark

Use well-lit pedestrian paths and watch slippery stone after rain. Keep children close near height changes at the water's edge. Concert crowds or temporary road closures can affect light-rail access; check [official light-rail notices](https://www.krtc.com.tw/KLRT/).
