## Love River Bay or downtown?

Downtown Love River offers a narrower scene framed by bridges and buildings. Near the harbour, Love River Bay has wider water and sky, helping you frame the setting sun and city silhouette. The sun's position changes with the month; no fixed viewpoint is guaranteed to put the sun directly in your photograph.

## A simple evening walking sequence

Start at **C10 Glory Pier**, walk around the exterior of Kaohsiung Music Center and examine the light across the water. Continue towards **C11 Love Pier**. If comfortable, extend to **C12 Dayi Pier-2** and capture the harbour at blue hour.

## Camera equipment and technique

A wide-angle lens captures the Music Center and open water; a longer lens compresses layers of distant bridges and buildings. Brace your camera in strong wind, and do not risk unstable positions beside the water to set up a tripod.

## Cloudy or rainy weather

Heavy cloud may hide the sun, but breaks in the clouds and wet surfaces can create other colours and reflections. Stop exposed waterfront photography during thunderstorms or strong winds; consider a [rainy-day route](/en/itineraries/rainy-day/) instead.
