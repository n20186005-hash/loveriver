## Before the performance

With two hours to spare, have a meal near the venue and walk a short distance around Love River Bay. Avoid a detour to upstream Heart of Love River. Check admission queues, restricted areas and event traffic controls before choosing a viewpoint for the Music Center buildings and harbour.

## Leaving after the show

Allow extra time for crowds to reach the light rail. Follow venue stewards and temporary signs; a station that is convenient on an ordinary day may have changed access arrangements during an event. If your onward connection is time-sensitive, plan a transport alternative.

## Enjoy the night view without rushing

The harbour can look especially attractive after dark, but do not stop in the middle of a crowded exit route for photographs. Move to an open public viewing area, and skip the detour if it would jeopardise your return train.

## Verify transport and event details

Check [Kaohsiung Music Center](https://kpmc.com.tw/) for the programme and venue notices, and [Kaohsiung Light Rail](https://www.krtc.com.tw/KLRT/) for current services.
