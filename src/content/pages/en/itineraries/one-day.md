## Morning | Begin with the city's history

Visit the **Kaohsiung Museum of History** area to learn about the city's waterfront past. Check the museum's opening hours and exhibitions. Walk nearby streets rather than immediately travelling to another river section.

## Lunch | Recharge in Yancheng

Choose a restaurant or local snack stop in Yancheng. Keep time for a seated break, especially in hot weather.

## Afternoon | Pier-2 and Dagang Bridge

Explore the converted port warehouses and art installations around Pier-2, then continue toward Dagang Bridge and the waterfront. Indoor exhibitions may charge admission separately.

## Dusk | Love River Bay

Enjoy the harbour opening, Music Center architecture and the changing colour of water and sky. Walk a one-way route between light-rail stops if practical.

## Evening | Downtown river and a possible cruise

Finish with illuminated bridges and the classic downtown atmosphere. If a Love Boat or gondola is operating, allow for a queue and verify boarding location before buying a ticket.

## Adjust for your energy level

This itinerary can run for **8–10 hours**. Skip the boat or one indoor stop if tired, and use public transport rather than insisting on walking every connection.
