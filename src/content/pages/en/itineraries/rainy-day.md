## Adjust for three levels of rain

For passing drizzle, begin indoors and leave a **20–30 minute** flexible window for waterfront reflections when the rain stops. For sustained rain, use MRT and light rail to move between covered venues. During thunderstorms or strong wind, avoid open piers, bridges and exposed waterfront paths.

## Indoor sections you can swap in

Consider the **Kaohsiung Museum of History**, selected Pier-2 exhibitions or indoor programmes around the Music Center. Each venue has its own opening time and ticket rules. A café or local meal in Yancheng can provide a comfortable break.

## After-rain night views

Wet streets and water can reflect city lights, but do not step onto slick waterside stairs or into a closed area for photographs. Keep a short route near a station and leave before the next storm.

## What about the cruise?

Treat a boat trip as optional in unstable weather. Check the operator's same-day notice and any ticket rebooking terms; see the [rain and boat service guide](/en/boats/rain-policy/).
