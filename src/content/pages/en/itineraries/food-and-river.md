## Dinner first or walking first?

If you want the river after dark, plan dinner in the **Yancheng** or **Qianjin** streets beforehand. Love River is a linear attraction and restaurants are not equally concentrated along every stretch. Eating in an established neighbourhood avoids a long back-and-forth search beside the water.

## Suggested route

Start at **O2 Yanchengpu** or **O4 Qianjin**, eat near the station, then walk to the downtown river for bridges and illuminated reflections. If you still have time, continue south towards Love River Bay. Choose a one-way route with a convenient end station.

## Reservations and opening times

Individual restaurants may close or change service days; check directly and allow time for queues. Do not assume night-market or restaurant opening times apply to all nearby businesses.

## Extend the evening

Consider a [Love Boat ride](/en/boats/love-boat/) or a short walk by the Music Center, but only after checking the actual boarding location and return transport. In thunderstorms, choose an indoor meal and museum instead.
