## Plan the light before picking stops

For sunset and evening photographs, arrive roughly **45 minutes before sunset** to choose a composition, then stay through blue hour. For birds, quieter paths and soft natural light, an early morning visit may be more rewarding.

## Suggested viewpoints

Use downtown Love River for boats beneath bridges and city reflections. At Love River Bay, frame the Music Center against open harbour water. Heart of Love River offers a different subject: twin lakes, vegetation and quieter paths, but requires a separate transit connection.

## Tides, water and bird activity

Water movement and bird sightings depend on conditions and season; do not promise any species or a fixed observation time. Maintain distance from wildlife and avoid disturbing habitat for a closer photograph.

## Equipment and etiquette

Use a wide lens for the harbour, a longer lens for compressed bridge views, and a secured phone or camera near water. Never block shared paths or enter restricted piers with a tripod. Protect equipment from salt air, wind and rain.

## Weather backup

Clouds may create unusual colour, but storms, lightning or strong wind are reasons to leave open waterfronts. Check the day's forecast and switch to indoor culture around Pier-2 if needed.
