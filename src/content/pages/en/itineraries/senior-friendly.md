## Start with transport and energy level

The most tiring part of a trip is often travelling between distant districts, not the attraction itself. Choose either downtown Love River with Yancheng or Love River Bay with the Music Center. Check the walking distance from the exact station against each traveller's mobility.

## A relaxed sequence

Arrive during the cooler morning or late afternoon, take a short riverside walk, rest indoors or at a café, then enjoy a second brief viewpoint if everyone feels comfortable. A taxi or public transport may be preferable to a long return walk.

## Boats and accessible paths

Boarding conditions, steps and assistance can vary by operator or pier. Ask staff before buying if anyone needs help boarding. Choose level pavements rather than waterside steps, and check the accessibility of station lifts and toilets.

## Rain and heat backup

Skip an outdoor section during thunderstorms or extreme heat. Indoor museums, exhibitions or a meal can replace it without spoiling the day. See the [accessible route](/en/itineraries/accessible/) for further principles.
