## Route overview

Start from MRT **O4 Qianjin**, reach the downtown river and move south towards **Love River Bay**. This one-way route avoids doubling back. You can either spend time on a sightseeing boat or cover more distance on foot; doing both at leisure may take longer than two hours.

## 0:00–0:20 | Qianjin to the waterfront

Navigate to the downtown river around Ambassador Pier, orient yourself and check the next boat departure if interested. Photograph a bridge and identify which riverbank makes your onward walk easiest.

## 0:20–1:00 | Cruise or walk

If a boat is available at a convenient time, allow for ticketing and the roughly 25-minute ride. Otherwise, follow the waterfront and enjoy reflections, gardens and river traffic from the bank.

## 1:00–1:35 | Continue towards the bay

Head south towards the wider harbour-mouth section. Look for a safe marked crossing and stay on public pedestrian paths. Add a brief stop at Holy Rosary Cathedral if it fits your pace.

## 1:35–2:00 | Finish at Love River Bay

See Love Pier and the Music Center area. If possible, time this part for twilight or city lights, then leave by the nearest light-rail station. Check the operator for current services and the actual departure pier.

## Rainy-day version

Shorten exposed walking, skip unsafe waterside sections in storms and consider an indoor stop at the Museum of History or Pier-2 instead. Never assume a cruise will operate in adverse weather.
