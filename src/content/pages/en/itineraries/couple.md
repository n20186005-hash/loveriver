## Keep the date relaxed

The best two-person itinerary leaves space for conversation and unplanned pauses. One sunset viewpoint, one boat choice and a comfortable meal can be more memorable than checking off every attraction.

## Part 1 | Sunset at Love River Bay

Start near **C10 Glory Pier** about an hour before sunset. Walk around the Music Center and harbour. The light changes across the water, while the wide promenade offers room for unhurried photographs.

## Part 2 | A downtown boat ride

Move to the classic river section and choose either a **gondola** for intimate atmosphere or the **Love Boat** for guided sightseeing. Verify the current boarding position, operating schedule and fare beforehand. Plan for possible waiting time.

## Part 3 | Dinner and riverside walk

Choose a restaurant around Qianjin or Yancheng based on your final transport connection. After eating, take a short, well-lit stroll by the bridges rather than retracing the whole waterfront.

## Small reminders

Bring a light layer if it is breezy by the water. During rain or major events, switch to an indoor dining and cultural outing rather than insisting on a boat departure.
