## Three planning principles

Keep the outing in one area, select a station with an accessible route and restroom options, and allow a convenient early exit. Do not attempt to cover downtown, the bay and the upstream lakes on one continuous walk.

## Suggested start and finish

For classic river views, consider the accessible route from MRT O4 or O2 to the nearby waterfront. For a modern harbour scene, start around **C11 Love Pier**. Check the station's current lift status and pedestrian crossings first.

## Level paths and potential barriers

Some pavements are level, but waterfront steps, bridge ramps, rough paving and intersections vary. Choose marked, accessible pedestrian routes rather than descending waterside stairs. A photo location is not worth an unsafe detour.

## Rest and pacing

Plan short sections with seated breaks, water and nearby indoor facilities. Decide in advance where to return to transit. Travelling companions should agree on a pace based on the least mobile person.

## Weather and live conditions

Avoid prolonged summer midday sun, slippery surfaces after rain and exposed waterfronts during storms. Temporary construction can change accessible access: use on-site signs and staff guidance.
