## Which section to choose

First-time solo visitors may enjoy the bridges and boat scenes downtown. For striking buildings and wider harbour views, choose Love River Bay. Both are accessible by public transport. Heart of Love River is also pleasant, but do not transfer upstream on impulse without checking your return route.

## Half-day example

Arrive in daylight, explore Yancheng or the Museum of History, have a relaxed meal and move towards the river as lights come on. Keep a specific station or transport exit in mind rather than wandering indefinitely along the bank.

## Safe photography alone

Stay on illuminated paths, avoid isolated waterfront stairs at night and keep your belongings secure while framing a shot. Never lean over railings or balance a device above open water. Respect restricted harbour infrastructure.

## Backup for the return

Check the last MRT and light-rail services, special-event crowds and weather before setting out. If conditions change, shorten the walk and leave from a convenient public stop.
