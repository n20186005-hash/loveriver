## Step 1 | Choose the scenery

For sightseeing boats, illuminated bridges and riverside reflections, go **downtown**. For harbour panoramas and Kaohsiung Music Center, choose **Love River Bay**. For two linked lakes and greener paths, visit **Heart of Love River** upstream. All have water scenery but different transit starting points.

## Step 2 | Choose your transport

Use **O4 Qianjin** for the classic cruise area, **C10–C12** for the bay, and **C24** for Heart of Love River. There is no single station called “Love River.” Search a specific landmark, especially if you intend to board a boat.

## Step 3 | Decide how long to stay

An hour is enough for a pleasant walk in one area. Two hours can connect downtown and the bay if you keep stops short. With three hours, consider a boat ride, dinner or a museum rather than trying to visit all three river sections.

## Step 4 | Check the day’s changes

Look up weather, special events, light-rail service and operating notices for boats or museums. Keep an indoor alternative for thunderstorms and leave a buffer for transport home.
