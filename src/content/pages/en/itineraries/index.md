## Choose by available time

- **[Two hours](/en/itineraries/2-hour/):** Walk downtown to Love River Bay, selecting either a cruise or a longer walk.
- **[Half day](/en/itineraries/half-day/):** Yancheng, Pier-2, Dagang Bridge, the bay at sunset and optional night views.
- **[Full day](/en/itineraries/one-day/):** Museums, neighbourhood food, art warehouses, harbour and classic river lights.
- **[MRT day trip](/en/itineraries/mrt-day-trip/):** Follow metro and light-rail connections without a car.

## Choose by who is travelling

- **[Families](/en/itineraries/family/):** A short boat option, rest breaks and easy transport.
- **[Couples](/en/itineraries/couple/):** Sunset, gondola atmosphere and dinner.
- **[Senior travellers](/en/itineraries/senior-friendly/)** and **[accessible visits](/en/itineraries/accessible/):** One area at a time with shorter walks.
- **[Solo travellers](/en/itineraries/solo-traveler/):** Well-lit paths, safe photography and flexible return options.
- **[Strollers](/en/itineraries/stroller/):** Level routes and family-friendly pacing.

## Principles shared by every itinerary

Love River has several distinct sections, not one entrance. Choose the attraction first, then its nearest station. Check current sailing, exhibition and transit notices, and allow time for weather or event-related changes.
