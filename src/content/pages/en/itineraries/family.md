## Principles for families

Do not measure success by distance covered. Plan one accessible waterfront area, food and toilet breaks, and perhaps one short boat trip. Use public transport to avoid a tiring return on foot.

## Stop 1 | Get oriented downtown

Begin at **O4 Qianjin** and walk to the classic riverbank. Watch the boats, bridges and lights. Explain where the safe pedestrian edge is and keep children within sight near the water.

## Stop 2 | Choose a Love Boat sailing

Check operating status and ticket categories for children. A guided boat can be an enjoyable family experience, but allow time for the queue and staff instructions. Skip it if the weather is poor or a child needs a rest.

## Stop 3 | Love River Bay—or head home

If everyone is comfortable, extend towards Love River Bay for the Music Center's architecture and broader waterfront. Otherwise, leave from your nearest convenient transit stop. There is no need to complete an entire prescribed route.

## Safety and comfort

Avoid long exposed stretches at summer midday, bring water and snacks, and choose level pavement when using a stroller. Check on-site closure notices and current light-rail services.
