## Why this sequence works

Moving from downtown Love River towards Yancheng and the harbour reduces unnecessary transfers. Heart of Love River is farther upstream, so this route deliberately leaves it out and gives you time to enjoy each stop.

## Morning | Qianjin and the classic river

Take the Orange Line to **O4 Qianjin** and walk around the bridges and Ambassador Pier. A boat may fit if current sailings and queues allow, but walking the banks is an equally valid start.

## Midday | Eat in Yancheng

Continue towards **O2 Yanchengpu** for neighbourhood streets and lunch. Check the opening times of individual food businesses before making a detour.

## Afternoon | Pier-2 and Dagang Bridge

Browse the art warehouses and public installations. Continue to Dagang Bridge, keeping in mind its opening movements and access rules can vary.

## Dusk and evening | Love River Bay

Use **C10–C12** to approach the wide harbour and Music Center, enjoy changing light, and leave from a convenient light-rail stop. Confirm the last service and allow additional time after events.
