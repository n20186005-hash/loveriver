## Which river section suits a stroller?

Downtown Love River, the bay and Heart of Love River all offer public paths, but ramps, bridge surfaces, crossings and steps vary. Choose a relatively level area close to a suitable station instead of treating the entire river as one continuous stroller route.

## A comfortable pace

Allow time for shade, drinks, feeding and toilets. Walk a short section with an easy return or onward transit connection, and stop when the child is tired. In hot weather, aim for morning or evening.

## Can a stroller go on a boat?

Boarding and storage rules depend on the vessel and operator. Ask directly whether a stroller can be taken aboard, must be folded or must remain at the pier before purchasing tickets.

## Safety near water

Keep a hand on the stroller and do not park it on a slope or close to unguarded edges. Avoid wet waterside stairs and crowded boarding areas; use marked pedestrian crossings and follow staff instructions.
