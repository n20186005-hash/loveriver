## Which area is best at sunrise?

For quieter greenery, consider the Heart of Love River area; for a recognisable urban river channel, choose downtown; and for a wide harbour skyline, visit Love River Bay. Lighting, maintenance and access differ by section, so follow on-site notices.

## Choosing a jogging route

Pick a level, familiar section with a straightforward return. Stay alert to cyclists and other pedestrians, and do not assume all waterfront paths remain continuous during works. Carry water even if the early air feels cooler.

## Photography in soft morning light

Look for low-angle light on bridges, a calm water surface and reflections between buildings. A longer lens can capture layered river views, while a wide lens shows greenery and the paths. Do not approach wildlife or block commuters for a photograph.

## Water and transport

Check the day's first MRT or light-rail service if arriving before the usual visitor hours. Bring sun protection as the morning warms and plan a nearby café or indoor rest rather than walking for hours in rising heat.
