## Route overview

Link the older Yancheng streets with the converted cultural warehouses at Pier-2, then enjoy the modern harbour architecture at Love River Bay. An optional downtown cruise can finish the evening; do not force it if the day is running late.

## 13:30 | Lunch in Yancheng

Choose a restaurant around the neighbourhood and take time to explore the streets. Check business opening times independently.

## 14:30 | Pier-2 warehouses

Visit the outdoor installations and any indoor exhibitions you find interesting. Individual galleries may have admission charges or separate hours.

## 16:00 | Dagang Bridge and the bay

Walk toward Dagang Bridge and the harbour-side promenade. Bridge operation and access may vary; do not plan around an unverified demonstration time.

## 17:00 | Sunset and blue hour

Pause near Glory Pier, Love Pier or the Music Center as the light changes. Sunset direction and timing depend on the season.

## 18:30 | Downtown river after dark

If comfortable, continue to the classic river section for illuminated bridges or a boat, subject to operating conditions. Otherwise, depart by light rail and MRT.

## Transport finish

Check the nearest useful stop and last service before dark, especially after a cultural event.
