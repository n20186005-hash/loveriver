## Is Love River free?

The public riverside walks do not charge admission. Boats, museums, special exhibitions and some events have separate tickets.

## Which station should I use?

For the downtown sightseeing-boat area, use Orange Line O4 Cianjin or O2 Yanchengpu. For Love River Bay, take the light rail to C10 Glory Pier, C11 Love Pier or C12 Dayi Pier-2. For Heart of Love River, use C24.

## Are Love River, Love River Bay and Heart of Love River the same place?

No. The downtown section is the classic river-cruise area; Love River Bay is the wider harbor near the estuary; Heart of Love River is a more northerly, two-lake ecological waterfront. They are not served by one station.

## Is daytime or evening better?

For a first visit, arrive before sunset to see the paths in daylight and enjoy both sunset and night lights. Families and first-time navigators benefit from some daylight; evening is ideal for reflections.

## Love Boat or gondola?

Choose Love Boat for narrated sightseeing and recognizing landmarks. Choose a gondola for the smaller craft, rowing and singing atmosphere. Trips are commonly around 25 minutes, but schedules and sales depend on the operator's notices.

## Do boats operate in rain?

Not always. Weather, water level, wind and river events can interrupt service. Check the operator on the day of departure.

## Is it suitable for children and older visitors?

Yes, with shorter walking sections, rest breaks, toilets and protection from midday heat. Using the light rail between sections is less tiring than walking the entire downtown-to-harbor route.

## Is Love River safe at night?

Main visitor stretches generally have lighting and other walkers, but this remains an open waterfront. Stay on the primary paths, supervise children, keep behind barriers and avoid isolated, dimly lit areas.

## Can I cycle?

Several riverside stretches are suitable for cycling, including routes around Heart of Love River. Slow down or walk the bicycle in busy pedestrian areas and follow posted signs.

## How long should I stay?

Allow 1.5–2 hours for a simple downtown walk, half a day for a cruise plus Love River Bay, or a full day if adding Dagang Bridge and Pier-2.
