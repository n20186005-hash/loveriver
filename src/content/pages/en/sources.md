## How we verify travel information

This guide prioritises official tourism pages, public transit agencies, venue operators and the original listing for each place. Boat timetables, admission, exhibitions and transport arrangements can change. Check the linked operator immediately before travelling.

## Official sources

- [Kaohsiung Travel – Love River](https://khh.travel/en/attractions/detail/232): city tourism information about the river and its surroundings.
- [Kaohsiung City Shipping Co.](https://kcs.kcg.gov.tw/): Love Boat sailings, fares and service notices.
- [Kaohsiung Rapid Transit Corporation](https://www.krtc.com.tw/): MRT and light-rail stations, routes and service information.
- [Kaohsiung Music Center](https://kpmc.com.tw/): venue, programme and visitor information at Love River Bay.
- [Pier-2 Art Center](https://pier2.org/): cultural exhibitions and outdoor installations.
- [Taiwan Tourism Administration](https://www.taiwan.net.tw/): national visitor information.
- [Love River on Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/SwMZaznF1f4iXwAz9): place location and visitor-submitted information.

## Photos and credits

Some pages display site-hosted photographs, with the page-specific source and credit alongside the image where available. Images taken from third-party collections remain subject to the source's stated terms. The presence of an image does not imply endorsement by an attraction operator.
