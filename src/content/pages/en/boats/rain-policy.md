## What matters when it rains

Operating decisions depend on more than rainfall. Lightning, wind, visibility, water conditions and equipment can all influence safety. A weather forecast helps your planning but cannot replace the boat operator's real-time decision.

## Three checks before going to the pier

1. Read the operator's latest sailing notice and ticket information.
2. Check short-term forecasts, especially thunderstorm and strong-wind warnings.
3. If already at the pier, ask staff whether tickets are being sold and when the next update is expected.

## If you already bought a ticket

Keep your confirmation and follow the vendor's specific instructions about rebooking or refunds. Different boat operators or booking channels may apply different rules; do not assume a generic policy covers your ticket.

## Indoor alternatives

Consider the Kaohsiung Museum of History, exhibitions around Pier-2 or a meal in Yancheng. Only return to the exposed waterfront when the weather is safe. See the [rainy-day itinerary](/en/itineraries/rainy-day/).
