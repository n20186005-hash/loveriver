## Two boats, two experiences

The **Love Boat** suits first-time visitors and families who want a guided look at riverside landmarks. The **gondola** is smaller, with rowing and singing creating a more romantic mood. Both are commonly described as roughly **25-minute** experiences, though actual routes, queues and ticketing vary.

The [side-by-side comparison](/en/boats/cruise-comparison/) helps decide based on atmosphere, group size and departure area.

## What to check before leaving

Confirm the day's **boarding station**, next sailing, current fares, payment method and weather-related notices. A fixed operating-hour listing does not guarantee every trip is running. The official operator's current announcement takes precedence.

## How to fit a cruise into your walk

Start near the downtown Ambassador Pier area, check the next available trip and walk along the banks while waiting. After disembarking, continue towards the bridges or Love River Bay for night views. Allow **45–60 minutes or more** for the entire boat stop rather than only the time on the water.

## Do you need to reserve?

Booking and walk-up arrangements depend on the service and date. Check the operator before travelling, particularly on holidays or event evenings. Keep your chosen boat's confirmation separate from a different operator's ticket.

## If sailings are suspended

Rain, wind, lightning, waterway events and equipment issues may affect service. A riverside walk or nearby indoor museum can provide a useful alternative. Read the [rain and cancellation guide](/en/boats/rain-policy/).
