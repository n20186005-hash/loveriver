## Three things to confirm before purchase

First, verify the **boarding station**: Love River has multiple piers, so a generic river pin is insufficient. Second, check the **day's actual sailings**, which can be affected by weather, equipment and events. Third, review **passenger categories**: children's, senior and concession rules must come from the current operator information.

## Buying at the pier and waiting

Look for today's ticket counter and meeting instructions, and confirm accepted payment methods and the next available departure. On busy evenings, leave time for purchasing, queuing and walking. Do not book a restaurant table just minutes after your expected disembarkation.

## How to avoid an outdated fare

Do not rely on a price copied from an old travel article. Use the operator's published current fares and ask staff about discounts or bundled offers. Different services, including the gondola, may not use the same price structure.

## With children or older travellers

Confirm boarding assistance, available seating and eligibility for any discounted ticket in advance. Allow more time for steps, ramps and queues, and follow crew safety instructions while aboard. For the precise meeting point, see [where to board](/en/boats/boarding-location/).
