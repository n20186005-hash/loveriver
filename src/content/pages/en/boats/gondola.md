## Who will enjoy it most?

The attraction is less about seeing as many landmarks as possible and more about being close to the water in a small boat, with rowing and a singing atmosphere. Couples, anniversaries and people who have already tried a conventional tourist boat may especially enjoy it.

## Where to board

The tourism listing places gondola activity near **Hedong Road and Minsheng Road** in the downtown section. Confirm the day's ticket, check-in and boarding positions with the operator, since these can change. This is separate from the harbour-side Love Pier.

## Ride duration and timing

Allow about **25 minutes on board**, plus time for ticketing, boarding and any queue. A twilight departure may combine the remaining sky colour with emerging city lights; actual operation is subject to the day's conditions.

## Photography tips

Use a secured phone or camera, avoid leaning over the boat and accept that a moving vessel can blur long night exposures. For portraits, look for soft light and the riverbank behind the passengers.

## Gondola versus Love Boat

The gondola feels intimate and atmospheric; the **Love Boat** is generally better for an introductory guided city sightseeing experience. See the [full comparison](/en/boats/cruise-comparison/).

## After the trip

Continue along the downtown banks for illuminated bridges, or walk towards Love River Bay if you have additional time. Do not schedule an immediate restaurant booking without allowing for possible waiting time.
