## Why visitors arrive at the wrong pier

Love River covers a long waterway. “Love River Bay,” “Love Pier,” “Love Boat” and “gondola” are different places or services. A map search for the river alone can lead far from your assigned boarding area.

## Check after booking

Read the boat type, boarding station, meeting time and location on your confirmation. If buying at a counter, confirm where the actual boarding pier is. Events and operating arrangements can move queues or sailings.

## Choose your transport destination

For the classic downtown Love Boat experience, start with **MRT O4 Qianjin** or **O2 Yanchengpu** and navigate to **Love Boat Ambassador Pier** when applicable. For a gondola, check the operator's location around Hedong Road and Minsheng Road. **Love Pier (C11)** belongs to the harbour-side Love River Bay area and is not interchangeable with those downtown boarding points.

## Families and tight schedules

With children, older companions or limited time, allow a buffer for finding the ticket counter, joining the queue and boarding. Verify the departure location with staff rather than relying on a generic map pin. See the [Love Boat guide](/en/boats/love-boat/).
