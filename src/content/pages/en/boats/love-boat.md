## Is the Love Boat right for you?

For a first visit to Love River, a short guided ride helps identify bridges and landmarks without planning a lengthy walking route. It is easy to combine with a two-hour or half-day outing. If you prefer a smaller, romantic ride, compare it with the [gondola](/en/boats/gondola/).

## Getting to Ambassador Pier

Use **O4 Qianjin** or **O2 Yanchengpu** as an MRT starting point, then navigate to the actual Love Boat boarding station. Confirm your ticket's station name before leaving: Love River Bay and Love Pier are different waterfront destinations.

## How to read operating information

Check the shipping company's latest sailing notices and ticket arrangements, not only a business-hours listing on a map. Weather, water conditions or special events may suspend or alter departures.

## How much time to reserve

The cruise is commonly around **25 minutes**, but allow additional time to find the counter, purchase tickets, wait and board. On busy evenings, keep your next appointment flexible.

## Which side offers the best view?

Both sides can show bridges and waterfront architecture; perspective changes as the boat turns. Avoid crowding or standing in unsafe places for photographs. Keep your camera secured and follow crew instructions.

## Where to go afterwards

Walk the downtown banks, look for night reflections or continue towards Love River Bay and the Music Center. For current prices and ticket rules, see the [ticketing guide](/en/boats/tickets/).
